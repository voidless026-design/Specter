# Vehicle notes

The car is a 2016 diesel estate. Service is due every 20,000 kilometres or
twelve months, whichever comes first. Last service was at 148,000.

Tyre pressure is 2.4 bar front and 2.6 rear when loaded. The spare is a
space-saver rated to 80 km/h.

The engine takes 5W-30 and about 5.2 litres with a filter change.

Insurance renews in August. The policy number is in the folder in the
kitchen drawer, not in the car.

The jack points are behind the front wheel arches and ahead of the rear
ones; there are notches in the sill to line up with.
