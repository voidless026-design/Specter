# Data retention policy

Operational logs are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. This
covers application logs, access logs and job output.

Customer records are retained for seven years after the account closes,
which is the statutory minimum for financial records in this jurisdiction.

Backups run nightly and are held for 35 days. Restoring from a backup older
than 35 days is not possible, so anything that must survive longer belongs
in the archive, not in backups.

Deletion requests are honoured within 30 days. Records under a legal hold
are exempt until the hold lifts, and the requester is told that this is why.

Anything containing payment card data is purged after 12 months regardless
of the above.
