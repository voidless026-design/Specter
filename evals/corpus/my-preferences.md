# Preferences and standing instructions

I take coffee black, no sugar. Tea with milk, no sugar.

I work best in the morning and would rather not have calls before 9am or
after 5pm.

When you give me directions, use distances in kilometres and temperatures in
Celsius.

I prefer short answers. If something needs detail, give me the short answer
first and offer the detail.

My home town is Melbourne. When I ask about the weather without saying
where, I mean there.
