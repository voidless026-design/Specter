# Foraging and food

Food is the least urgent survival need - most people manage three weeks
without it - so never take a risk on an unidentified plant.

## The universal edibility test

The test takes a day and is a last resort. Separate the plant into parts,
test one at a time. Rub a piece on the inside of the wrist and wait fifteen
minutes. If there is no reaction, hold a piece against the lip, then on the
tongue, then chew without swallowing, waiting between each. Swallow a small
amount and wait eight hours. It does not detect slow-acting toxins, which is
why it is a last resort rather than a method.

## Reliable foods

Cattail roots, acorns after leaching out the tannins, dandelion in full, and
pine inner bark are all edible across wide ranges. All grasses are
technically edible though most are indigestible.

## Insects

Insects are the highest-return protein in most environments. Avoid anything
brightly coloured, hairy, or that smells strongly, and cook everything.
