# Essential knots

## Bowline

The bowline forms a fixed loop at the end of a rope that will not slip or
tighten under load, which is what makes it the standard rescue knot. It can
be untied after bearing weight, unlike most loop knots. It is not secure
under repeated loading and unloading, so back it up with a stopper for
anything critical.

## Clove hitch

The clove hitch secures a rope to a post or rail and adjusts easily. It
slips under a sideways pull, so it suits temporary work rather than
anchoring.

## Figure-eight

The figure-eight is a stopper knot that prevents a rope running out through
a device or a hole. Tied as a figure-eight on a bight it makes a strong loop
that is easy to inspect visually, which is why climbers prefer it to the
bowline.

## Taut-line hitch

The taut-line hitch grips under tension but slides along the standing part
when released, making it the right knot for guy lines on a tent.
