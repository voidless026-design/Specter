# Field first aid

## Bleeding

Direct pressure stops most bleeding. Press hard with whatever is cleanest
and do not lift it to check - clot disruption restarts the bleed. A
tourniquet goes two to three inches above the wound, never over a joint, and
is tightened until the bleeding stops, which will hurt. Write down the time.
Modern practice does not loosen a tourniquet periodically; that was
abandoned decades ago.

## Hypothermia

Shivering stops when hypothermia becomes severe, which reads as improvement
and is the opposite. Get the person dry, insulated from the ground, and
into shelter. Warm sweet drinks help if they are alert enough to swallow.
Do not rub cold limbs and do not give alcohol.

## Burns

Cool a burn with running water for twenty minutes. Do not use ice, butter or
toothpaste. Cover loosely with cling film or a clean non-fluffy cloth.
