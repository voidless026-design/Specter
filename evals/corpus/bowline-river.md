# Mooring a boat

Tying up a boat is a matter of matching the line to the load and the tide.

## Bow and stern lines

The bow line runs from the front of the boat forward to the dock, and the
stern line from the back aft. Together they stop the boat surging along the
dock. Spring lines run diagonally and do the real work of holding position.

## Cleats

A line is made fast to a cleat with a round turn and then figure-eight
turns, finished with a locking hitch only if the line will not need casting
off in a hurry. Never tie a knot that jams to a cleat.

## Tide

Allow slack for the tide range or the boat hangs or submerges. In a large
range, rig the lines long and diagonal so the geometry changes less as the
water moves.
