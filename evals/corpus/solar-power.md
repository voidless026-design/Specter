# Off-grid solar

## Panels

Panel output is quoted at standard test conditions, which are optimistic.
Expect roughly 75 to 85 percent of rated output in good real conditions and
much less in cloud. Panels lose efficiency as they heat up, roughly 0.4
percent per degree above 25 Celsius, which is why a cool bright day
outperforms a hot one.

## Batteries

Lead-acid should not be discharged below 50 percent if you want reasonable
cycle life. Lithium iron phosphate tolerates 80 to 90 percent depth of
discharge and lasts several times longer, at higher upfront cost.

## Controllers

An MPPT controller tracks the panel's maximum power point and typically
recovers 20 to 30 percent more energy than a PWM controller, especially in
cold or low light. PWM is only sensible for very small systems.

## Wiring

Undersized cable is the most common fault. Voltage drop over a long low
voltage run is substantial; size for under 3 percent drop.
