# Navigation without GPS

## Map and compass

Magnetic north and true north differ by an angle called declination, which
varies by location and drifts year to year. Ignoring it puts you roughly one
degree off per degree of declination, which compounds over distance. Set the
declination on the compass housing if it adjusts.

## The sun

The sun rises roughly east and sets roughly west, exactly so only at the
equinoxes. The shadow-stick method is more precise: mark the tip of a
vertical stick's shadow, wait fifteen minutes, mark it again. The line
between the marks runs roughly west to east, first mark west.

## Stars

In the northern hemisphere, Polaris sits within one degree of true north.
Find it by following the two stars at the end of the Big Dipper's bowl. In
the southern hemisphere there is no equivalent star; use the Southern Cross,
extending its long axis four and a half times to find the south celestial
pole.
