# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored as glucose,
and is the basis of almost every food chain on Earth.

## Light-dependent reactions

These take place in the thylakoid membranes. Chlorophyll absorbs light,
mainly in the blue and red parts of the spectrum, which is why plants appear
green - green light is reflected rather than absorbed. Water is split,
releasing oxygen as a by-product, and the energy is captured as ATP and
NADPH.

## The Calvin cycle

In the stroma, ATP and NADPH from the light reactions drive the fixation of
carbon dioxide into glucose. The enzyme RuBisCO catalyses the first step and
is thought to be the most abundant protein on Earth.

## C4 and CAM

Plants in hot dry climates use variants. C4 plants such as maize concentrate
carbon dioxide before fixing it. CAM plants such as cacti open their stomata
only at night, which loses far less water.
