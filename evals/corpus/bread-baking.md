# Bread baking

## Hydration

Hydration is the weight of water as a percentage of the weight of flour. A
standard loaf sits around 65 percent; ciabatta and focaccia run 75 to 85 and
are correspondingly harder to handle. Higher hydration gives a more open
crumb.

## Fermentation

Yeast produces carbon dioxide and alcohol. A slower, cooler ferment develops
more flavour because it gives enzymes time to break starch into sugars.
Overnight in the fridge is the easiest way to get this.

## Gluten

Kneading aligns gluten strands into a network that traps gas. The same
effect happens without kneading given enough time, which is the basis of
no-knead recipes.

## Oven

Steam in the first ten minutes keeps the crust soft long enough for the loaf
to expand, then dry heat sets the crust. A covered pot achieves this without
any special equipment.
