# Signalling for rescue

Three of anything is the international distress signal: three fires, three
whistle blasts, three flashes. Rescuers are trained to read repetition as
deliberate.

## Visual

A signal mirror is visible up to fifty kilometres in good conditions and
weighs almost nothing. Aim by holding two fingers up at the target and
walking the reflected spot of light across them. Ground-to-air symbols
should be at least three metres across with maximum contrast; a large X
means require medical assistance and a V means require assistance.

## Audible

A whistle carries far further than a voice and costs no energy. Three
blasts, pause, repeat. Shouting exhausts you and dries your throat.

## Smoke

Green vegetation or rubber on a hot fire makes dense white or black smoke.
Contrast is what matters - white smoke against dark forest, dark smoke
against snow.
