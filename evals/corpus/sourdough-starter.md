# Sourdough starter

A starter is a stable culture of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria in
flour and water. It takes about a week to establish and years to kill.

## Starting one

Mix equal weights of wholemeal flour and water. Discard half and feed with
fresh flour and water daily. Activity usually appears around day three,
stalls around day four as the bacterial population shifts, and becomes
reliable by day seven.

## Feeding

A 1:5:5 ratio of starter to flour to water at room temperature peaks in
about eight hours. Colder or a smaller proportion of starter takes longer. A
starter kept in the fridge needs feeding weekly rather than daily.

## Signs

A healthy starter roughly doubles and smells sharp and yoghurty. Acetone or
nail polish smells mean it is hungry, not dead. A grey liquid on top is
alcohol; pour it off and feed.
